Clomate is a historical weather dashboard for travellers.
Users enter a country, date range (MM-DD to MM-DD), years 
to compare (2020–2025), and up to 10 place names. The app 
geocodes each place via Open-Meteo geocoding API and fetches 
historical daily weather via Open-Meteo archive API. Shows 
temperature, feels-like, and rainfall charts per location 
with cold-alert badges. Built for cold-sensitive travellers 
from warm climates planning trips anywhere in the world.
